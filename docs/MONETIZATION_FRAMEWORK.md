# NexVR Engine — Complete B2C & B2B Money Generating Framework

A comprehensive, dual-engine commercial roadmap designed to scale NexVR to **$500,000+ ARR in Year 1** while operating strictly within established interoperability fair-use legal standards (*Sega v. Accolade*, *Sony v. Connectix*) and publisher-friendly compliance boundaries.

---

## 🏛️ The Golden Foundation: The Interoperability Compliance Rule

Before looking at prices, the entire business relies on **one non-negotiable rule**:

> **The Core Injector and All Community Game Profiles must remain 100% FREE FOREVER.**

* **Why? (The Luke Ross Precedent)**: Luke Ross monetized game-specific VR mods behind a paid Patreon paywall, triggering DMCA disputes from Take-Two (*GTA V*) and CD Projekt (*Cyberpunk 2077*) for commercializing unauthorized derivative game modifications.
* **The NexVR Defense**: Because our core injector runtime and community profiles are 100% free, we never sell access to any publisher's intellectual property. We monetize **Platform Cloud Infrastructure** (cloud profile sync, DirectML neural compute models, streamer camera tools) and **Official B2B Developer Middleware** licensed directly to game studios.

## 🏢 ENGINE 2: B2B (Selling to Studios & Hardware Makers)

The B2B side delivers large **upfront cash lump-sums** ($25,000 to $100,000 per contract).

### Track A: Mid-Tier Game Studios (The "Official VR Port" Plugin)
Game studios with flat PC titles want to sell their games on Meta Quest and SteamVR, but building a VR port from scratch costs **$2,000,000** and takes **18 months**.

* **The Product**: **NexVR Studio SDK** (`nexvr_sdk.dll` + `nexvr_sdk.h` + Unity/Unreal plugin).
* **The Promise**: *"Turn your existing PC game into an official VR release in 2 weeks with just 4 lines of C++ code."*
* **Pricing Options**:
  1. **Direct Middleware License**: **$35,000 upfront** + **5% royalty** on all VR game copies sold.
  2. **Zero-Risk Co-Publishing**: **$0 upfront** + **50/50 net revenue share** on the VR edition. (If the game sells 25,000 VR copies at $20 = $500k, NexVR gets **$250,000**).

### Track B: XR Glasses & Headset OEMs (XREAL, Rokid, Pimax, Viture)
Makers of AR/XR glasses have sleek hardware, but users complain there are **no AAA games to play**.

* **The Product**: **NexVR Spatial OEM Bridge**.
* **The Promise**: Allows any buyer of their XR glasses to plug into a PC and immediately play 50,000 Steam games in spatial 3D with head tracking.
* **Pricing**: **$50,000 – $100,000 annual license** or **$3.00 per device bundled**.

### B2B Revenue Math (Year 1 Target):
* **3 Indie/AA Studio Licenses** (@ $35,000) = **$105,000**
* **VR Game Sales Royalties** = **$50,000**
* **1 OEM Hardware Partnership** (e.g. XREAL or Pimax) = **$100,000**
* **👉 B2B Total Year 1 Revenue: $255,000**
