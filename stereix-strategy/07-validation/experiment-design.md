# 🧪 Top 3 Experiment Detailed Designs

> 📌 **TL;DR**: 
> - **Exp 1**: Hardware pilot with 10 testers on Quest 3 / Index (Target: $\ge 8$ launch in < 60s).
> - **Exp 2**: 15s viral video proof on TikTok/Shorts (Target: 5,000 views, 50 signups).
> - **Exp 3**: $39 Founder Pass pre-order email to waitlist (Target: $\ge 15$ paid orders).

---

## 🎯 Experiment 1: The "Founder's Ring" Hardware Pilot

* **What it tests**: Does v0.1.98 launch smoothly on a clean tester PC without manual troubleshooting?
* **Who**: 10 PCVR gamers with Quest 3 / Index who own *Sekiro* or *Mortal Shell*.
* **The 3 Feedback Questions**:
  1. *How many seconds from opening launcher to standing in VR?* (Target: < 60s).
  2. *Did you experience any motion sickness or double-vision?* (Target: 0 nausea).
  3. *Would you pay $39 for 10 top games like this?*
* **Pass / Fail**: PASS if $\ge 8/10$ launch cleanly; FAIL if $> 2$ testers experience DLL crashes.

---

## 🎯 Experiment 2: The 15-Second Short-Form Video Proof

* **The Formula**: "Library $\rightarrow$ Space" (Flat Screen vs. Inside the Game).
* **Storyline**:
  - *Sec 0–3*: Flat monitor running *Sekiro*. *"Why am I playing on a flat desk?"*
  - *Sec 4–8*: Clicking "Launch in VR" in NexVR $\rightarrow$ snapping Quest 3 on.
  - *Sec 9–15*: Real in-headset footage. Boss swings katana. Player parries in 3D scale with curved HUD.
* **Pass / Fail**: PASS if $\ge 5,000$ views and $\ge 50$ waitlist signups in 7 days.
