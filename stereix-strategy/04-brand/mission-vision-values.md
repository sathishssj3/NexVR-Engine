# 🌟 Mission, Vision, & Operating Values

> 📌 **TL;DR**: 
> - **Mission**: Eradicate modding friction and turn standing inside your favorite games into a 1-click button.
> - **Vision**: A world where the artificial wall between flat monitors and spatial VR no longer exists.
> - **4 Values**: 1-Click Integrity, 11.1ms Frame Budget Law, Radical Honesty, and Cryptographic Rigor.

---

## 🎯 Mission Statement
> **"To eradicate the modding friction of virtual reality and make standing inside your favorite PC games as effortless as pressing Play."**

---

## 👁️ Vision Statement
> **"A gaming universe where every digital world ever created can be stepped into instantly, comfortably, and beautifully."**

---

## ⚖️ The 4 Core Decision-Making Values

1. **One-Click Integrity (Zero Tinkering)**:
   * If a feature requires the user to open a text editor or edit an `.ini` file, **it is not ready to ship**.
2. **The 11.1ms Law (Frame Pacing is Sacred)**:
   * We will never add an unoptimized effect that drops frames below 90 FPS or induces nausea. Stable 90 FPS beats flashy ray-tracing.
3. **Radical Honesty & Account Protection**:
   * We never claim "every game works." We explicitly block injection into anti-cheat online games to protect user accounts from bans.
4. **Cryptographic & Code Rigor**:
   * All binaries are Authenticode signed; all OTA manifests are Ed25519 verified. Zero developer machine paths leak into production.
