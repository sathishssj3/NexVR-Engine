Pipeline

How It Works

Five stages between a flat executable and a world you can lean into.

  1. 01

    Detect

    The launcher fingerprints the running process, identifies the graphics API and engine, and loads the matching profile from the compatibility index.

    Process scan

  2. 02

    Hook

    A signed DLL is injected into the target and intercepts the swapchain, device context, and draw submission at the boundary — no game files are modified on disk.

    DX11 / DX12

  3. 03

    Render

    The view and projection matrices are recovered and duplicated per eye. The scene is submitted twice against the headset's exact frustum, with per-eye culling to keep the cost sane.

    Stereo pass

  4. 04

    Track

    Head pose from OpenVR/OpenXR is folded into the camera each frame, with late-latching before submit so rotation stays locked to your neck, not to the game's tick rate.

    6DoF · late latch

  5. 05

    Play

    Frames go to the compositor with reprojection and per-title comfort options — snap turn, world scale, HUD depth — tunable from the in-headset overlay.

    Compositor

Answers

Frequently Asked

Will this get me banned from multiplayer games?

Assume yes for any title with kernel-level anti-cheat. NexVR injects into the rendering pipeline, which most anti-cheat systems cannot distinguish from a hostile hook. The launcher refuses to attach to titles on our anti-cheat blocklist, and we recommend single-player only.

Does it modify game files?

No. Everything happens in memory at runtime. Nothing on disk is patched, so uninstalling NexVR returns the game to exactly its original state.

What hardware do I actually need?

Stereo rendering roughly doubles GPU load. An RTX 3060 / RX 6600 is a realistic floor for older titles at 90 Hz; modern AAA games want a 4070-class card or better. Per-title recommendations live on each compatibility card.

Is it true 6DoF, or just a screen floating in front of me?

True stereoscopic 6DoF wherever the game's view matrix can be recovered — you get real depth and positional head movement. Titles where only rotation can be resolved are marked Partial in the index, and behave closer to a 3D theater.

Do motion controllers work?

Controllers are mapped to gamepad and mouse-look input by default, so aiming follows the game's own scheme. Native motion-controller aiming exists per profile for a small set of verified titles and is expanding.

Is the source available?

The injection core, renderer, and tracking layer are open source under an MIT licence. The curated profile index and the in-headset overlay are the paid layer.
