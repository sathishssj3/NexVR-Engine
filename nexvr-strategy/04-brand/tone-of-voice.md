# 🗣️ Tone of Voice & Lexicon Guide

> 📌 **TL;DR**: 
> - **Personality**: Precision-Obsessed, Radically Direct, Gamer-First, and Effortlessly Simple.
> - **Golden Rule**: Never use the words "Mod," "Hack," or "Injector" in marketing. Use **"Engine," "Platform," and "1-Click VR Console."**

---

## 🎭 Brand Personality Traits

* **Precision-Obsessed**: We quote real numbers (*"11.1ms frame budget", "OpenXR 1.0.34"*), not vague marketing fluff.
* **Radically Direct**: We tell gamers the plain truth (*"Don't play online with this or you will get banned"*).
* **Gamer-First**: We share the goosebumps of fighting a boss in *Sekiro* in 3D scale.
* **Effortless & Clean**: Short sentences, punchy verbs, zero clutter.

---

## 🚫 Avoided Words vs. Preferred Words

| ❌ AVOID THESE (Amateur/Sketchy) | ✅ USE THESE (Premium/Console) | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Mod / Hack / Script | **Engine / Runtime / Platform** | "Mod" implies amateur, buggy, and free. |
| Hook / Inject / Detour | **1-Click Launch / VR Mode** | Technical jargon intimidates casual gamers. |
| Preset script / Config file | **Verified Hero Profile** | Sounds curated, valuable, and tested. |
| Fake 3D / Z-buffer trick | **True Stereoscopic 3D / 6DOF** | Accurately describes spatial depth. |
| Retinal overlay / Screen HUD | **Curved Floating World-Space HUD** | Emphasizes the nausea-free innovation. |
