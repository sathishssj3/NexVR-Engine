# 🎨 Brand Personality & Visual Direction

> 📌 **TL;DR**: 
> - **Brand Archetype**: **The Magician** (Transforms flat games into 3D worlds) + **The Ruler** (Brings order and control to modding chaos).
> - **Visual Identity**: High-tech cockpit aesthetic. Deep space obsidian (`#0A0B0E`) with laser crimson (`#FF2E56`) and electric cyan (`#00F0FF`).
> - **Typography**: `Chakra Petch` (Display), `Inter` (Body), and `JetBrains Mono` (Telemetry).

---

---

## 🎨 Color Palette & Visual Tokens

* ⬛ **Obsidian Black (`#0A0B0E`)**: Primary OLED-black background.
* 🟥 **Laser Crimson (`#FF2E56`)**: Primary accent. Represents raw performance and adrenaline.
* 🟦 **Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`)**: Secondary accent. Represents spatial OpenXR tracking precision.
* ⬜ **Pure White (`#F0F4F8`)**: Crisp, readable typography.

## 👁️ Emotional Transformation

* **Before NexVR**: Frustrated, exhausted by `.ini` files, sick from retina-glued HUDs.
* **After NexVR**: Awestruck, relieved, thrilled to experience their games in full 3D scale.
