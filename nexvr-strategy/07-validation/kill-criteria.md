# 🛑 Kill & Pivot Criteria (Sunk-Cost Protection)

> 📌 **TL;DR**: 
> - 6 hard, measurable triggers that tell the founder when to **stop, pivot, or shut down** instead of burning time and money.
> - **Anti-Cheat Zero Tolerance**: Even 1 confirmed ban triggers immediate auto-injection shutdown.

---

## 🚫 The 6 Kill / Pivot Conditions

### 1. Crash Failure Rate > 33% (Hardware Pilot)
* **Trigger**: If more than 5 out of 15 testers experience unrecoverable DLL crashes.
* **Action**: **STOP Marketing**. Revert to private alpha and fix GPU hook compatibility.

### 2. Video Proof Failure (< 1k Views, < 10 Signups)
* **Trigger**: If 5 distinct TikTok/Shorts clips generate under 1,000 views and under 10 signups.
* **Action**: **PIVOT Content Angle**. Shift focus from "platform utility" to game-specific titles (*"How to play Elden Ring in VR"*).

### 3. Commercial Rejection (< 5% Conversion on $39 Pass)
* **Trigger**: If fewer than 5 people buy the $39 pass out of 100 qualified waitlist members.
* **Action**: **PIVOT Business Model**. Shift directly to B2B Studio SDK licensing ($15k fee to game publishers) or freemium $19.99 pricing.

### 4. Sickness / Refund Rate > 15%
* **Trigger**: If more than 8 out of the first 50 buyers request refunds citing nausea.
* **Action**: **HALT Sales Immediately**. Recalibrate FOV and depth unprojection math before resuming.

### 5. Verified Anti-Cheat Account Ban (FATAL TRIGGER)
* **Trigger**: A single verified report of an anti-cheat ban while using NexVR.
* **Action**: **IMMEDIATE SHUTDOWN of Auto-Injection**. Hardcode mandatory offline process decoupling or shift strictly to official developer-endorsed studio ports.

### 6. Acquisition Cost (CAC) Exceeds $20
* **Trigger**: If blended CAC exceeds $20 against a $39 pass (LTV:CAC drops below 2:1).
* **Action**: **KILL Paid Ads**. Rely strictly on organic word-of-mouth and creator rev-share.
