> 📌 **Executive Overview**:

## 💵 1. Financial & Accounting Short Forms

| Short Form | Full Form | Meaning & Context in NexVR |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ACV** | **Annual Contract Value** | The average annualized revenue generated per enterprise contract (e.g. $150,000/yr for defense flight sim installations). |
| **ARR** | **Annual Recurring Revenue** | Predictable, normalized annual recurring subscription and royalty revenue generated by the business. |
| **MRR** | **Monthly Recurring Revenue** | Predictable monthly revenue from SaaS subscriptions ($9.99/mo Pro users). |
| **ASC 606** | **Accounting Standards Codification Topic 606** | The US GAAP standard governing revenue recognition from contracts with customers. |
| **GAAP** | **Generally Accepted Accounting Principles** | The standardized rules and practices for financial reporting in the United States. |
| **COGS** | **Cost of Goods Sold** | Direct costs attributable to the production and delivery of goods/services (e.g. payment gateway fees, CDN hosting). |
| **OPEX** | **Operating Expenses** | Ongoing expenditures required to operate the company (R&D payroll, creator marketing, legal/administrative). |
| **CapEx** | **Capital Expenditure** | Funds used by a company to acquire, upgrade, and maintain physical assets (e.g. hardware test benches). |
| **EBITDA** | **Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization** | Primary metric of core operational cash profitability before non-operating accounting adjustments. |
| **FCF** | **Free Cash Flow** | Operating cash flow minus capital expenditures; the actual cash available for reinvestment or distribution. |
| **LTV** | **Lifetime Value** | The total gross profit predicted from a customer over the duration of their relationship with the product. |
| **CAC** | **Customer Acquisition Cost** | The total sales and marketing cost required to acquire one single paying customer. |
| **LTV:CAC**| **Lifetime Value to Customer Acquisition Cost Ratio**| Efficiency multiple of sales (NexVR operates at **4.65 : 1**; >3:1 is considered elite). |
| **B2C** | **Business-to-Consumer** | Commercial transactions conducted directly between the company and retail gamers. |
| **B2B** | **Business-to-Business** | Commercial transactions between NexVR and other corporations (game studios, headset OEMs, defense primes). |
| **TAM** | **Total Addressable Market** | Total global revenue opportunity if 100% market share was achieved ($124.1M for Steam PCVR). |
| **SAM** | **Serviceable Available Market** | The segment of the TAM targeted by the company's product and channel ($31.5M for RTX 3070+ campaign gamers). |
| **SOM** | **Serviceable Obtainable Market** | The realistic beachhead portion of SAM that can be captured in early phases ($975,000 for 25k users). |
| **QSBS** | **Qualified Small Business Stock** | US Internal Revenue Code Section 1202 provision granting **100% tax-free capital gains** up to $10M+ on stock held 5+ years. |
| **IRC** | **Internal Revenue Code** | The domestic statutory tax code of the United States. |
| **IPO** | **Initial Public Offering** | The first sale of stock by a private company to the public financial markets (NASDAQ). |
| **SEC** | **Securities and Exchange Commission** | US federal agency regulating securities markets and public corporate disclosures. |
| **S-1** | **SEC Form S-1** | The initial registration filing required by the SEC for a private company before going public. |
| **SOX 404**| **Sarbanes-Oxley Act Section 404** | US federal law mandating strict internal financial accounting controls and external auditing for public companies. |
| **FDIC** | **Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation** | US government agency insuring bank deposits (up to $250,000 per institution). |
| **SIPC** | **Securities Investor Protection Corporation** | US non-profit protecting investors against the failure of brokerage firms. |
| **ICS** | **IntraFi Cash Service** | Banking network that splits large corporate deposits across hundreds of banks to secure tens of millions in FDIC insurance. |
| **DSO** | **Days Sales Outstanding** | Measure of the average number of days that it takes a company to collect payment after a sale. |
| **BOM** | **Bill of Materials** | Comprehensive list of raw materials, assemblies, and software components required to manufacture a hardware device. |

## 🎮 2. Virtual Reality, Extended Reality & Spatial Computing Short Forms

| Short Form | Full Form | Meaning & Context in NexVR |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **VR** | **Virtual Reality** | Completely simulated, immersive digital environment blocking out the physical world. |
| **AR** | **Augmented Reality** | Digital graphics, holograms, and spatial windows overlaid directly on the physical world. |
| **XR** | **Extended Reality** | The universal umbrella term encompassing VR, AR, and Mixed Reality (MR). |
| **MR** | **Mixed Reality** | Hybrid environment blending physical reality with interactive digital objects (passthrough camera feeds). |
| **6DOF** | **Six Degrees of Freedom** | Tracking rotational movement (pitch, yaw, roll) AND positional movement (forward/back, left/right, up/down). |
| **3DOF** | **Three Degrees of Freedom** | Tracking rotational orientation only (pitch, yaw, roll) without positional movement in space. |
| **OpenXR** | **Open Cross-Platform Standard for XR** | Royalty-free open standard developed by the Khronos Group unifying access to VR/AR hardware across vendors. |
| **HUD** | **Heads-Up Display** | In-game visual UI (health bars, ammunition, crosshairs, maps). NexVR extracts this onto a 3D curved floating mesh. |
| **FOV** | **Field of View** | The observable angular world that is visible at any given moment on display optics (e.g. 110° horizontal FOV). |
| **IPD** | **Inter-Pupillary Distance** | The physical distance between the centers of the pupils of the eyes (standard virtual baseline is 64mm). |
| **FPS** | **Frames Per Second** | The frequency at which consecutive images are captured or displayed (90 FPS = 11.1ms per frame). |
| **Hz** | **Hertz** | Unit of frequency equal to one cycle per second (90 Hz display = 90 frames refreshed per second). |
| **VRS** | **Variable Rate Shading** | GPU feature that varies the shading rate across different regions of a frame (enabling foveated rendering). |
| **ETFR** | **Eye-Tracked Foveated Rendering** | Rendering the tiny focal area where the eye is looking in 4K, and coarsening peripheral pixels to save 40% GPU power. |
| **OLED** | **Organic Light-Emitting Diode** | Display technology with self-illuminating pixels producing true infinite contrast and pure blacks. |
| **LCD** | **Liquid Crystal Display** | Standard flat-panel display technology utilizing backlighting. |

## 💻 3. Graphics Engineering, Shaders & System Architecture

| Short Form | Full Form | Meaning & Context in NexVR |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DX11** | **Microsoft DirectX 11** | High-performance 3D graphics API widely used in PC games (e.g. *Sekiro*, *God of War*). |
| **DX12** | **Microsoft DirectX 12** | Low-level, explicit 3D graphics API providing bare-metal GPU control (e.g. *Elden Ring*, *Cyberpunk 2077*). |
| **DXGI** | **DirectX Graphics Infrastructure** | Low-level subsystem managing swapchains, display outputs, and GPU adapters on Windows. |
| **HLSL** | **High-Level Shader Language** | Microsoft's proprietary shading language used to write compute, vertex, and pixel shaders for DirectX. |
| **FXC** | **Effects Compiler** | Microsoft's legacy shader compiler targeting Direct3D 9, 10, and 11 compute shader model 5.0 (`cs_5_0`). |
| **DXC** | **DirectX Shader Compiler** | Modern LLVM-based open-source compiler targeting Direct3D 12 shader model 6.0+ (`cs_6_0`) and SPIR-V. |
| **SPIR-V** | **Standard Portable Intermediate Representation** | Cross-API binary intermediate language for graphics shaders and compute kernels in Vulkan and OpenCL. |
| **PE** | **Portable Executable** | The standard file format for executables, DLLs, and drivers on 32-bit and 64-bit Windows operating systems. |
| **DLL** | **Dynamic Link Library** | Shared library of executable code and data that can be dynamically injected into running processes on Windows. |
| **SDK** | **Software Development Kit** | Collection of software development tools, header files (`nexvr_sdk.h`), and libraries for third-party developers. |
| **API** | **Application Programming Interface** | Formal set of subroutine definitions, protocols, and tools for building software applications. |
| **ABI** | **Application Binary Interface** | Low-level binary interface between two program modules (e.g. C-linkage `extern "C"`). |
| **MinHook**| **Minimalistic x86/x64 API Hooking Library** | Lightweight detour library used by NexVR to redirect graphics function calls (`Present()`, `DrawIndexed()`). |
| **DirectML**| **Direct Machine Learning** | High-performance, hardware-accelerated DirectX 12 machine learning API by Microsoft running on any GPU. |
| **ONNX** | **Open Neural Network Exchange** | Open-source ecosystem and runtime format for interoperable deep learning models (`.onnx`). |
| **NeRF** | **Neural Radiance Field** | Deep learning method representing 3D volumetric scenes from 2D images as continuous radiance fields. |
| **3DGS** | **3D Gaussian Splatting** | Real-time radiance rendering technique using 3D Gaussian ellipsoids to represent photorealistic scenes at 120+ FPS. |
| **NPU** | **Neural Processing Unit** | Specialized hardware microprocessor designed specifically for accelerating machine learning algorithms. |
| **GPU** | **Graphics Processing Unit** | Specialized electronic circuit designed to manipulate and alter memory to accelerate image creation. |
| **CPU** | **Central Processing Unit** | Primary electronic processor executing basic arithmetic, logic, and system control instructions. |
| **DSP** | **Digital Signal Processor** | Specialized microprocessor optimized for real-time mathematical signal processing (used in smart glasses silicon). |
| **VRAM** | **Video Random Access Memory** | Dedicated high-bandwidth memory used by the GPU to store textures, depth buffers, and framebuffers. |
| **DMA** | **Direct Memory Access** | Feature of computer systems allowing hardware subsystems to access main system memory directly without CPU. |
| **BCI** | **Brain-Computer Interface** | Direct communication pathway between an electrical brain/neural signal sensor and an external computing device. |
| **EMG** | **Electromyography** | Electrodiagnostic medicine technique for evaluating electrical activity produced by skeletal muscles. |
| **OTA** | **Over-The-Air** | Wireless transmission and automated deployment of software updates, manifests, and profiles. |
| **CDN** | **Content Delivery Network** | Geographically distributed network of proxy servers providing fast delivery of software assets (Cloudflare). |
| **R2** | **Cloudflare R2 Object Storage** | S3-compatible cloud storage offering zero egress bandwidth fees. |
