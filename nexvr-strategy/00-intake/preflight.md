# 🚦 Pre-Flight Sanity Check & Prior Failures

> 📌 **TL;DR**: 
> - **No Dominant Monopolist**: Existing options are legacy software (VorpX), engine-restricted (UEVR), or Patreon mods (LukeRoss).
> - **Prior Failure Lesson**: Never claim "every game works instantly" (VorpX's mistake). Anchor around **10 Verified Hero Masterpieces**.
> - **Anti-Cheat Red Line**: Strict single-player/offline enforcement is mandatory to protect user accounts from bans.

---

## 🔍 The 3 Sanity Checks

### 1. Dominant Incumbent Check
* **Status**: **Open Space (No Monopolist)**
* **Why**:
  - **VorpX**: Outdated 2014-era UX, widely disliked for nausea.
  - **UEVR**: Free, but strictly limited to Unreal Engine (cannot touch *Sekiro*, *Elden Ring*, or Unity).
  - **LukeRoss**: $10/mo Patreon for ~10 games; uses AER ghosting that causes motion sickness.

### 2. Precedent Failure Check
* **Status**: **Lessons Learned**
* **The VorpX Trap**: VorpX claimed to support 700+ games. In reality, most games looked distorted and caused nausea, leading to community backlash.
* **The NexVR Rule**: Focus on **10 Guaranteed Hero Games** (*Sekiro*, *Mortal Shell*, *Hogwarts Legacy*, *Elden Ring*) with perfect comfort, and label the rest as experimental.

### 3. Anti-Cheat & Legal Instant-Kill Check
* **Status**: **Safe (with Hard Blacklist)**
* **The Threat**: Multi-player games with Easy Anti-Cheat or BattlEye will ban DLL injectors.
* **The Protection**: Hardcoded blacklist preventing injection into online games. Supported games (*Elden Ring*) are forced into offline mode.

---

## 🚩 Flag Summary

* 🔴 **Red Flag**: Never attempt injection into competitive multiplayer games.
* 🟡 **Yellow Flag**: Clearly communicate that non-hero games are experimental.
