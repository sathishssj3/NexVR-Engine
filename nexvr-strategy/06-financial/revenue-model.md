# 💵 Revenue Model & Pricing Mechanics

> 📌 **TL;DR**: 
> - **Primary Stream**: $39 Founder Lifetime Pass (introductory price, then $49).
> - **Secondary Stream**: $9.99/mo Pro Pass for Day-1 game profile releases.
> - **Enterprise Stream**: $15,000 B2B SDK license for game studios releasing official VR editions.

---

## 🏷️ The 3 Revenue Streams

### 1. Founder Lifetime Pass ($39.00 Introductory)
* **What it is**: 1-time payment unlocking the 10 Hero Masterpieces, bilateral compute filter, System Doctor, and permanent updates.
* **Target Conversion**: **4.5%** of free community downloads.
* **Net Revenue per Sale**: **$36.75** (after 5.5% payment processing).

### 2. Pro Monthly Pass ($9.99 / month)
* **What it is**: Subscription for power users wanting Day-1 profile drops on release days.
* **Average Retention**: 4.2 months. Average Net LTV: **$38.60**.

### 3. B2B Studio SDK License ($15,000 upfront + 10% rev-share)
* **What it is**: Licensing the NexVR SDK to indie/AA studios to ship official SteamVR/Quest editions.
* **Target**: 2 studio agreements in Year 2 ($30,000 net profit).
