### 🟢 Year 1 (2026 – 2027): The 1-Click Consumer Beachhead
> **Strategic Theme**: *"Eradicate Modding Friction & Prove Zero-Debt Unit Economics"*

* **🎯 The Master Goal**: Prove that PC gamers will pay $39 upfront for a 1-click VR launcher that replaces 2 hours of modding frustration.
* **📏 The Kill/Pass Metric**: **3,700 Founder Passes sold ($145k)** + **330 Pro Subscribers ($40k)** = **$185,000 Gross Revenue** with **0 Anti-Cheat bans**.
* **Quarterly Execution Roadmap**:
  - **Q1**: Launch closed "Founder's Ring" pilot (10 testers, clean PCs). Validate < 60s launch time.
  - **Q2**: Release v1.0 public build. Ship System Doctor pre-flight diagnostic and curved 3D floating HUD.
  - **Q3**: Launch viral creator rev-share pipeline (TikTok/Shorts + 3 VR YouTubers at 20% affiliate fee).
  - **Q4**: Expand verified profile catalog to the "Hero 25" games. Achieve $102k in net cash profit.
* **🚫 The Anti-Goal (What MUST NOT Happen)**:
  - Do NOT spend a single dollar on paid ads.
  - Do NOT support online multiplayer games with active anti-cheat (protect user accounts from bans).
  - Do NOT raise dilutive seed capital (protect 98% founder ownership).
* **💰 Financial Target**: $185,000 Revenue · $102,000 EBITDA · **Founder Net Worth: $1.47M**.

  - **Q2**: Sign first 2 indie game studios developing single-player RPGs/shooters.
  - **Q3**: Launch the Community Profile Marketplace (users sell custom profiles; NexVR takes a 30% platform cut).
  - **Q4**: Close OEM evaluation pilot with Bigscreen Beyond distributing 8,000 bundled licenses at $12.50/unit.
* **🚫 The Anti-Goal (What MUST NOT Happen)**:
  - Do NOT hire a bloated sales team. Keep engineering lean.
  - Do NOT lose focus on the consumer product; consumer groundswell is your B2B leverage.
* **💰 Financial Target**: $742,000 Revenue · $381,500 EBITDA · **Founder Net Worth: $5.70M**.
