# 🧠 Customer Psychology & Pain Map

> 📌 **TL;DR**: 
> - PCVR gamers spent **$2,000+ on hardware**, but their headsets collect dust due to lack of AAA games.
> - Their biggest frustration is **Modding Fatigue**: spending 2 hours setting up injectors instead of playing.
> - They hate monthly subscriptions for single-player games, but gladly pay **$39 for a 1-click lifetime pass**.

---

## 😫 The 4 Core Pains of the PCVR Gamer

### 1. Hardware Guilt
* **The Pain**: *"I spent $600 on a Quest 3 and $1,500 on an RTX 4070. After Half-Life: Alyx, there are no real AAA games to play."*
* **The Consequence**: Headset sits on a shelf gathering dust.

### 2. Modding Fatigue (The "Second Job")
* **The Pain**: Setting up mods requires editing `.ini` files, finding memory offsets, and tweaking 50 sliders.
* **The Consequence**: Gamers give up before they even start playing.

### 3. Motion Sickness & Eye-Strain
* **The Pain**: Legacy injectors glue 2D health bars directly to the player's eyes and distort the field of view.
* **The Consequence**: Nausea and headaches within 15 minutes.

### 4. The "Steam Update Breakage"
* **The Pain**: A 50MB Steam update drops, crashes the community mod, and the player is stranded mid-campaign.
* **The Consequence**: Anger and abandoned save files.
