## 🎯 3 Key Findings from Discovery

### 1. Gamers Pay for Time, Not Code
* Out of 12 interviewed enthusiasts, **8 said they would instantly pay $30–$50** for a tool that launches *Sekiro* in VR in 1 click without manual configuration.

### 2. FromSoftware Games are the Holy Grail
* Gamers love UEVR for Unreal games, but desperately want FromSoftware games (*Sekiro*, *Elden Ring*, *Dark Souls*) which UEVR cannot touch. This is NexVR's primary advantage.

### 3. The System Doctor Prevents Silent Drop-Off
* All 5 beta testers noted that the integrated **Pre-Launch System Doctor** eliminated startup crashes by fixing OpenXR and Windows Defender before launch.
